# Parc National Tsingy de Bemaraha - Landing Page  

This project is a **landing page** dedicated to the **Parc National Tsingy de Bemaraha**, a UNESCO World Heritage site in Madagascar. The landing page provides visitors with essential information about the park, including its unique landscapes, activities, species, and upcoming events.  

---

## 🌍 About the Park  

The **Tsingy de Bemaraha National Park** is one of Madagascar’s most remarkable natural wonders. Known for its sharp limestone formations called *Tsingy*, the park is home to unique biodiversity and stunning landscapes. It is a hub for ecotourism, adventure, and wildlife discovery.  

---

## ✨ Features  

- **Introduction Section**: Highlights the beauty and importance of the park.  
- **Activities Section**: Showcases available activities such as hiking, climbing, canoeing, and guided tours.  
- **Species Section**: Displays the endemic wildlife and plants found in the park.  
- **Events Section**: Presents cultural or natural events happening inside the park.  
- **Responsive Design**: Accessible on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
